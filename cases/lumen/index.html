§ III · A MANIFESTO

ON METHOD

Curation, not casino.

The current generation of image tools has the metabolism of a slot machine. Type a prompt, receive a pile, reroll, reroll again. The interface treats vision as a probability distribution and the user as a gambler. We watched art directors — actual ones — open these tools, generate forty images in ninety seconds, and close the tab. They were not impressed. They were tired.

Lumen is the opposite proposition. We believe a tool for image-makers should behave like a curator, not a croupier. It should read intent, select with confidence, and present a small, sequenced wall. It should fail in a particular way rather than succeed in a generic one. We are building Lumen for the people who already have an eye — who know precisely what they want, and refuse to look at the eight images they did not.
