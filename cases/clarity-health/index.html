      What Clarity does NOT do

      The lines we hold, on purpose.

      —

      No diagnosis

      We point toward possibilities, never conclusions. A diagnosis is a clinical act. We are not clinicians.

      —

      No prescriptions

      We will never recommend a treatment, a dose, or a drug. Decisions about medication belong to you and a physician.

      —

      No replacing your doctor

      We're a primer, not a practitioner. Clarity is what you read on the train to your appointment, not instead of it.

      Built with physicians

      Every answer is reviewed by clinicians who practice.

      Privacy, plainly

      Your symptoms stay yours.

      End-to-end encrypted

      Everything you write in Clarity is encrypted on your device before it leaves it. We can't read your history. Neither can your insurer.

      No ad targeting

      We don't sell health data. We don't run ads. Clarity is funded by a flat monthly subscription, full stop.

      Yours to delete

      One tap clears your entire history. We keep no shadow copies, no analytics tied to your identity, no exceptions.
